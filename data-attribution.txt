Fantasy Terminal — public data attribution and changes, October 16, 2026

This is a collection of separately attributed data portions, not a claim to
own or relicense third-party material. Private league information is excluded.

NFL statistics, rosters, play-by-play, draft data and snap records:
nflverse through nflreadpy; original sources include NFL and PFR.
https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data
https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/blob/main/LICENSE.md
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) as published by nflverse, subject to
any separately designated third-party material. Our changes: cutoff filtering,
exact identifier joins, recorded-appearance averages and readable summaries.

Expected fantasy points and derived opportunity/gap/forecast data portions:
ffopportunity, Joe Sydlowski and Tan Ho / ffverse, via nflverse.
https://ffopportunity.ffverse.com/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. Our changes: position-specific
PPR sums, per-appearance averages, scoring-source decomposition, conservative
gap weights and separately evaluated forecasts. Adapted expected-points data
are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Upstream version lineage is not independently
verified for each cached historical release. Historical revisions are possible.

Open receiving tracking:
NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse/nflreadpy. Target-weighted separation/cushion;
reception-weighted yards-after-catch above expectation. Weekly records only,
no season-total row used as a pre-cutoff input. Not all-routes-run metrics.
https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_nextgen_stats.html

Market data portion (also separately downloadable as market.json):
DynastyProcess.com / FantasyPros Expert Consensus Rankings.
https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data
GPL-3.0; full upstream license is market-license.txt. The adapter source is
market-source.txt. Changes: exact provider-ID mapping, current dated format
selection, tied within-position ranks/scores, and an allowlisted public subset.
These are consensus-derived references, not actual executed trades or dollars.
No KTC or Dynasty Daddy price feed is included. Market rank comparisons derived
from this portion retain the upstream GPL-3.0 license.

Draft ADP portion:
Fantasy Football Calculator, PPR / 12 teams, with original draft sample dates.
The official API documentation permits personal and commercial use and requests
attribution. Not a live in-season trade price. Unlisted players are not zero.
https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/
https://help.fantasyfootballcalculator.com/article/42-adp-rest-api

Full current routes-run counts are not redistributed. The sources checked did
not establish a reliable current feed with public reuse permission. Snap share
and targeted-pass separation are not silently substituted for routes run.
